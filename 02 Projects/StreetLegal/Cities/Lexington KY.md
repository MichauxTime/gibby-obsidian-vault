## Permit Stack (detailed)

### 1. Mobile Food Unit (MFU) Annual Registration
- **Agency:** Lexington-Fayette County Health Department (LFCHD), Environmental Health Division
- **Address:** 650 Newtown Pike, 2nd Floor, Lexington, KY 40508
- **Phone:** (859) 231-9791
- **Fee:** ~$100 (annual) — *[Inferred: consistent across secondary sources; fee amount not published on LFCHD website — verify at (859) 231-9791]*
- **How to apply:** In-person at 650 Newtown Pike; call to initiate process
- **URL:** https://www.lfchd.org/food-truck/
- **Prerequisites:** Signed commissary agreement, approved menu, equipment list, plan review documents
- **Renewal:** Annual, with renewal inspection
- **Processing time:** 3–6 weeks (plan review is the long pole)
- **Notes:** Must submit 48-hour advance location notifications for each operating day. Menu must be approved before permit is issued. Separate **temporary event permits** are available: $60 (1–3 days), $90 (4–7 days), $125 (8–14 days) — *[Verified: lfchd.org]*

### 2. Occupational License
- **Agency:** LFUCG Division of Revenue
- **Address:** 218 E. Main St., Lexington, KY 40507
- **Phone:** (859) 280-8300
- **Fee:** $100 initial application fee; ongoing 2.25% net profits tax (quarterly if under $53,333/yr revenue, monthly if over)
- **How to apply:** In-person or by mail
- **URL:** https://www.lexingtonky.gov/working/business-licensing-taxes/how-start-business-lexington
- **Prerequisites:** State business registration complete
- **Renewal:** Annual
- **Processing time:** 1–2 weeks
- **Notes:** *[Verified: lexingtonky.gov]* This is both a one-time registration fee AND an ongoing quarterly profit tax. Budget for both.

### 3. Zoning Compliance Certificate
- **Agency:** LFUCG Division of Planning
- **Address:** 101 East Vine Street, Suite 700, Lexington, KY 40507
- **Phone:** (859) 258-3160
- **Fee:** Free (2-year certificate)
- **How to apply:** In-person
- **Prerequisites:** None
- **Processing time:** Same-day to 1 week
- **Notes:** *[Inferred: based on 2013 pilot ordinance; confirm current status with Division of Planning — call (859) 258-3160]* The 2013 food truck ordinance eliminated the old Certificate of Occupancy requirement and replaced it with this free certificate. Status may have changed.

### 4. Kentucky Food Protection Manager Certification
- **Agency:** Kentucky CHFS; must use ANSI-accredited program (ServSafe, Trust20, StateFoodSafety, etc.)
- **Fee:** ~$150 (course + exam)
- **How to apply:** Online through approved provider
- **URL:** https://www.chfs.ky.gov/agencies/dph/dphps/fsb/Pages/retailfoodprogram.aspx
- **Renewal:** Every 5 years
- **Processing time:** Pass the exam; certification immediate upon passing
- **Notes:** *[Verified: KY CHFS]* As of summer 2025 updates aligning with 2022 FDA Food Code, a Certified Food Protection Manager (CFPM) must be present during ALL hours of operation. This applies to every food truck handling unpacked food.

### 5. Fire Inspection
- **Agency:** Lexington Fire Department / Fire Marshal
- **Fee:** ~$50–$150 *(Inferred: typical range; confirm with Lexington Fire Dept.)*
- **How to apply:** Schedule with fire marshal after truck is equipped
- **Prerequisites:** Fire suppression system installed (if using fryers/grills), fire extinguishers mounted, proper ventilation
- **Notes:** If your truck requires a new fire suppression system (Ansul-type), budget an additional $1,500–$3,000 for installation. LFCHD publishes fire safety guides specifically for food truck operators at lfchd.org/food-truck/.

### 6. Kentucky Sales Tax Permit
- **Agency:** Kentucky Department of Revenue
- **Fee:** Free
- **How to apply:** Online at revenue.ky.gov
- **URL:** https://revenue.ky.gov
- **Renewal:** Not required (but keep account active)
- **Processing time:** 2–5 business days
- **Notes:** *[Verified: KY DOR]* 6% state sales tax on prepared food. No additional local sales tax in Lexington-Fayette County.

### 7. Kentucky Business Entity Registration
- **Agency:** Kentucky Secretary of State via Kentucky One Stop Business Portal
- **Fee:** $40 filing (LLC); $15 annual report
- **URL:** https://onestop.ky.gov
- **Renewal:** Annual report ($15)
- **Processing time:** ~1 hour online
- **Notes:** *[Verified: onestop.ky.gov]* Do this first — many other registrations reference your state registration. Sole proprietors can skip this but lose liability protection.

## Filing Path

**Week 1 — Foundation (run in parallel):**
1. Register business entity — Kentucky Secretary of State, onestop.ky.gov ($40 LLC)
2. Obtain Federal EIN — irs.gov (free, 15 min)
3. Begin commissary kitchen search; sign agreement early (required before health permit)
4. Enroll in ServSafe Manager certification

**Week 1–2 — Local Registrations:**
5. Apply for Occupational License — LFUCG Division of Revenue, 218 E. Main St. ($100)
6. Obtain Zoning Compliance Certificate — Division of Planning, 101 E. Vine St., Suite 700 (free; call ahead: (859) 258-3160)

**Week 2–3 — Health Department (longest step):**
7. Execute commissary agreement (signed, on commissary's letterhead)
8. Submit MFU application to LFCHD, 650 Newtown Pike, 2nd Floor — bring commissary agreement, menu, equipment list. Call (859) 231-9791 to initiate.
9. Wait for plan review (3–6 weeks)

**Week 3–5 — Fire & Vehicle:**
10. Install fire suppression system if needed
11. Schedule and pass Lexington Fire Dept. inspection
12. Register truck with KY Transportation Cabinet (vehicle registration)

**Week 5–8 — Final:**
13. Register for Kentucky Sales Tax permit at revenue.ky.gov (free, online, 2–5 days)
14. Pass LFCHD health inspection (physical truck inspection)
15. Receive MFU permit. Begin 48-hour advance notification for each operating location.

**Key dependency:** Commissary agreement → MFU application → health inspection → permit. The commissary must be locked in before health dept. process can begin.

## Where You Can Actually Operate

### Permitted Zones
- **Private property:** With landowner permission. Most reliable and flexible option.
- **On-street designated zones:** City has on-street food truck vending zones established under the 2013 ordinance pilot. Designated streets include portions of Limestone, Mill, Vine, East High, Elm Tree Lane, West Main, and West High Streets. Current zone maps: contact Division of Planning (859) 258-3160. *[Inferred: pilot zone list from 2013; verify current active zones]*
- **Brewery/distillery patios:** Private property with owner permission. Noted as the most reliable ongoing revenue channel by local operators.
- **Special events:** City-coordinated events, private events — handled separately.

### No-Vend Zones
- **100 feet from any open restaurant/business front door** — unless written approval from that business owner, or operating as part of a Special Event. *[Verified: ordinance text]*
- **100 feet from residential property line in Residential District** — unless written permission from HOA. *[Verified: ordinance text]*
- **Exact school proximity distance:** Not confirmed from official ordinance text — verify with LFUCG Division of Planning
- **University of Kentucky campus:** Not covered by city permits — requires separate vendor agreement with UK Dining Services or Office of Student Affairs

### Time Restrictions
- On-street vending zones: 7:00 a.m. to 10:00 p.m. *[Inferred: from ordinance text; confirm current hours]*
- No moving sales — truck must be stationary

### Downtown
- No separate special downtown permit beyond standard MFU, but must be in a designated on-street zone. Contact Division of Planning for current active zone map.

## Local Tips & Gotchas
- **48-hour advance notification is mandatory** — you must tell LFCHD where you'll be operating at least 48 hours in advance. You cannot decide the morning-of. Plan your week.
- **The 100-foot restaurant rule is real** — downtown Lexington has restaurants close together. Map your preferred spots against open restaurant locations before committing to a zone.
- **Food Truck Fridays is at capacity** — don't count on it for Year 1. Get on the waitlist now, build brewery/private event revenue in the meantime.
- **UK game days are gold** — Commonwealth Stadium draws ~61,000. Secure a vendor agreement with UK early; spots near campus perimeter on game days fill fast.
- **Brewery patio circuit** — Lexington's craft brewery scene (West Sixth, Country Boy, etc.) actively seeks food truck partners for weekly slots. This is the backbone of a stable schedule.
- **Keeneland timing** — Don't miss April and October. Set up near the course perimeter; the crowds spill out of the venue.
- **Commissary is non-negotiable** — Kentucky has no waiver process. No commissary agreement = no permit.
- **The 2013 ordinance is aging** — Some zone specifics and the exact operating hours may have been revised. Call Division of Planning at (859) 258-3160 before scouting locations.
