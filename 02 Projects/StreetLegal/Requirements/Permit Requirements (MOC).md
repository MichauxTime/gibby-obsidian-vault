# Permit Requirements (MOC)

The cross-cutting layer. Food-truck permit rules repeat in every jurisdiction with local variations — so instead of burying each rule inside 200+ city guides, each **requirement domain** gets one hub here that aggregates the rule across all states. Every city/state guide links *into* the relevant hub; the hub links back out to the jurisdictions that vary.

> Why this exists: Ben noticed the vault had requirements scattered per-city with no master connection point. These hubs are that connection point — they're also natural pillar-page / SEO and product-content sources.

## Requirement Domain Hubs
- [[Commissary Kitchen Requirements]] — the commissary/commercial-kitchen rule. Directly ties to our kitchen marketplace (the moat).
- [[Fire Code Requirements]] — fire marshal inspection, propane/suppression, clearances.
- [[Insurance Requirements]] — general liability, auto, commissary-required COIs, event COIs.
- [ ] Health Permits & Food Safety — health dept permit, food handler/manager certs (hub to build)
- [ ] Business Licensing & Tax — DBA/assumed name, state license, sales tax registration (hub to build)
- [ ] Mobile Vendor Permit & Zoning — vending permit, certificate of occupancy, parking/zoning (hub to build)

## How a hub works
- **Universal layer:** what's true almost everywhere (the rule, why it exists, the typical documents).
- **Variation layer:** how specific states/cities differ — links to the city/state guides where the specifics live.
- **Source of truth:** per-jurisdiction specifics stay in the city guides + auto-fill mappings + permit-grades; the hub aggregates and points, it doesn't duplicate.
