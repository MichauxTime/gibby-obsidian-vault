## Permit Stack (detailed)

### 1. Mobile Food Establishment License
- **Agency:** Central District Health (CDH) — Environmental Health Division
- **Fee:** $80/year (self-contained or licensed commissary) | $100/year (using unlicensed commissary) | $100 one-time plan review fee
- **How to apply:** Email application to EHApps@cdh.idaho.gov; application must be submitted at least 30 days before planned opening
- **URL:** https://cdh.idaho.gov/community-health/food-establishments/food-fees-forms-and-applications/
- **Prerequisites:** Completed application + floor plan + menu + Mobile Food Unit Operational Plan; commissary agreement (if using commissary)
- **Renewal:** Annual; late fee $35 (Jan 1–15) or $70 (after Jan 15); reinstatement $18
- **Processing time:** Allow 30+ days
- **Notes:** CDH covers Ada, Boise, Elmore, and Valley counties. Contact: 208-327-7499
- **Confidence:** Verified (.gov source)

### 2. City of Boise Vendor License
- **Agency:** City of Boise — City Clerk's Office
- **Fee:** $141/year or $70.75 semi-annual; $1,000 cash/surety bond (individual) or $5,000 bond (company)
- **How to apply:** Online at permits.cityofboise.org
- **URL:** https://www.cityofboise.org/departments/city-clerk/licensing/other/vendor-license/
- **Prerequisites:** CDH food establishment license; proof of insurance
- **Renewal:** Annual
- **Processing time:** Varies; contact City Clerk at (208) 972-8150
- **Notes:** 2024 ordinance eliminated per-employee background checks — now licenses the business entity
- **Confidence:** Verified (cityofboise.org)

### 3. Boise Fire Department — Mobile Food Preparation Vehicle Permit
- **Agency:** Boise Fire Department — Fire Prevention Division
- **Fee:** Not published online — call (208) 570-6559 to confirm
- **How to apply:** Online at permits.cityofboise.org → select "Fire Permit"
- **URL:** https://www.cityofboise.org/departments/fire/fire-prevention/permits/special-use-and-operations/mobile-food-preparation-vehicles-permit/
- **Prerequisites:** Vehicle must have cooking equipment producing smoke or grease-laden vapors
- **Renewal:** Annual; inspection required
- **Processing time:** Fire Dept contacts applicant to schedule inspection after application
- **Notes:** Inspection checklist covers suppression system, propane/fuel system, and ventilation
- **Address:** 333 N Mark Stall Pl, Boise, ID 83704
- **Confidence:** Verified (cityofboise.org); fee unverified — VERIFY before publishing

### 4. Idaho Sales and Use Tax Permit
- **Agency:** Idaho State Tax Commission
- **Fee:** Free
- **How to apply:** Online at tax.idaho.gov
- **URL:** https://tax.idaho.gov/online-services/business-registration/

- **Confidence:** Verified (.gov source)

### 5. Idaho Business Registration / DBA
- **Agency:** Idaho Secretary of State
- **Fee:** $25
- **How to apply:** Online at business.idaho.gov
- **URL:** https://business.idaho.gov/register-a-business/
- **Notes:** Not required if operating as sole proprietor under your legal name
- **Confidence:** Verified (.gov source)

### 6. Food Safety Manager Certification
- **Agency:** ANSI-accredited provider (ServSafe, Prometric, etc.)
- **Fee:** ~$150–$200 (exam + training materials)
- **How to apply:** Enroll with approved provider
- **Renewal:** 5 years
- **Notes:** At least one person on the truck must hold a valid certification; enforced by CDH during permit review
- **Confidence:** Inferred (standard Idaho CDH requirement)

## Filing Path

Follow this sequence — each step unlocks the next:

1. **Register your business** — Idaho Secretary of State DBA ($25) + Idaho Sales Tax Permit (free)
2. **Get Food Safety Manager Certification** — at least one person on the truck
3. **Apply to Central District Health** — submit application, floor plan, menu, Operational Plan at least 30 days before opening ($80 + $100 plan review)
4. **Schedule Boise Fire Dept inspection** — apply online at permits.cityofboise.org for Mobile Food Preparation Vehicle Permit; fire dept will contact you for inspection
5. **Obtain City of Boise Vendor License** — apply online at permits.cityofboise.org ($141/yr + bond)
6. **Secure commissary agreement** (if not fully self-contained) — CDH must approve the commissary
7. **Apply for specific location permits** — park vending permit ($27/day) or verify downtown medallion placement with City Clerk

## Where You Can Actually Operate

### Permitted Zones
- **Private property** — anywhere in the city with property owner permission (most flexible option)
- **City parks (pilot program):** Fort Boise Park, Julia Davis Park, Kristin Armstrong Municipal Park, Molenaar Park, Veterans Memorial Park
  - Season: April 15 – November 15
  - Daily fee: $27
  - Limit: One vendor per park per day (first-come, first-served)
  - Must be fully self-contained (no power/water hookups)
  - Apply: https://www.cityofboise.org/departments/parks-and-recreation/park-licenses-and-permits/vending-application-and-information/
- **Downtown streets outside BID** — authorized street medallion locations; contact City Clerk (208) 972-8150

### No-Vend Zones
- **Downtown BID ban:** Food trucks banned from street parking within the Downtown Business Improvement District
  - BID boundary: 5th to 13th Streets (E–W) and State to Myrtle Streets (N–S)
  - Exception: Private property within BID is allowed with owner permission
- **Sports/Athletic facilities:** Soccer, baseball, lacrosse, football facilities excluded from park vending (except Willow Lane Athletic Complex)
- **School proximity:** No specific distance restriction confirmed — VERIFY with City Clerk or Planning Dept

### Time Restrictions
- Park vending season: April 15 – November 15 only
- No confirmed operating hour restrictions for private property vending
- Downtown medallion placement subject to City Clerk rules

One of the strongest recurring food truck revenue opportunities in the city. Organized by Downtown Boise Association. URL: https://downtownboise.org/events/alive-after-five
- **Foodfort at Treefort Music Fest** — Annual 5-day festival (typically March), Downtown Boise. Foodfort is the curated food component — strong application-based selection. URL: https://treefortmusicfest.com/fort/foodfort/
- **Boise Farmers Market** — Weekly downtown market; food vendors welcome under standard vending license
- **Corporate / tech catering** — Boise's tech employer base (Micron, HP, Clearwater Analytics) creates strong weekday lunch demand

- **Peak season:** April–October (park permits active, outdoor events running)
- **Off-peak:** November–March (no park vending; focus on private events, indoor events, catering)

## Local Tips & Gotchas
- **The downtown BID ban is the #1 pain point** for new Boise food truck operators. Don't plan your launch strategy around downtown street spots — the ban has been in effect since ~2002 and shows no sign of lifting. Focus on private property deals and event circuits instead.
- **SIFTA is worth joining early.** The Southern Idaho Food Truck Association (sifta.org) is active and connects members to events, permitting updates, and the local truck community. 67+ members as of 2025.
- **Park vending is competitive.** The one-vendor-per-park-per-day rule and the April–November season make parks a supplement, not a foundation. Arrive early and have backup plans.
- **Boise is one of the fastest-growing cities in the US.** The Treasure Valley population boom means new neighborhoods, new office parks, and new event venues every year — scout emerging areas in Meridian, Eagle, and Nampa (which have their own permit requirements).
- **CDH plan review takes 30+ days.** Don't sign a lease or buy a truck before starting the CDH permit process. The 30-day minimum submission window is a hard constraint.
- **Commissary self-sufficiency is possible and cheaper.** Unlike some cities, Boise doesn't require a commissary if your truck is genuinely self-contained. A quality tank setup saves $250–$800/month in ongoing commissary fees.
- **Treasure Valley sprawl.** Meridian (~130,000 pop) and Nampa (~100,000 pop) are adjacent cities with their own permit requirements. A multi-city strategy covering Boise + Meridian + Nampa requires separate licenses but opens a much larger market.
