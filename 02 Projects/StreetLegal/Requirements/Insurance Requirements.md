# Insurance Requirements

Cross-jurisdiction hub for insurance requirements on food trucks — the one that ties together cities, commissaries, AND events.

## Universal layer (true almost everywhere)
- **General liability** coverage is the baseline almost everyone asks for (cities, commissaries, events, private bookings).
- **Commercial auto** insurance on the vehicle itself.
- **Workers' comp** once there are employees (state-driven threshold).
- Proof is delivered as a **Certificate of Insurance (COI)**, often naming an **additional insured** (the city, commissary, venue, or event organizer).

## Variation layer (what differs by jurisdiction & counterparty)
- City permit minimums vary (common GL limits like $1M/$2M, but not universal).
- **Commissaries** frequently require their own COI naming them as additional insured — ties to [[Commissary Kitchen Requirements]].
- **Events / festivals** almost always require an event-specific COI with the organizer as additional insured — ties to event content.
- Some states/cities require auto/workers'-comp proof at permit time; others don't check.
